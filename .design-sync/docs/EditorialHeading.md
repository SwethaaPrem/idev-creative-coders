The site's display heading: light-weight Outfit with tight tracking, revealed line by line through a mask. Choose `size` by role: `hero` (home headline), `page` (page titles, CTA), `section` (section titles), `card` (titles inside cards and panels). Pass the copy as `lines`; a line can be JSX, e.g. `<span className="text-accent">emphasis</span>`. Colour comes from `text-text-primary`; override with `className`.
