A project's details as a glass panel floating over or beside its visual: title, description, technologies, metrics and the case-study link. `variant="card"` stacks vertically (overlap a visual's corner on desktop); `variant="bar"` spreads across a wide strip pinned to the bottom of a full-width visual. Takes a whole `project` object (see `window.IDEV.projects`). Needs a Router in context.
